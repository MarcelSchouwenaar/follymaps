# New York City Highlights

Discover the most iconic locations in New York City, from the serene beauty of Central Park to the vibrant energy of Times Square. This curated collection showcases the diversity and character that makes NYC one of the world's greatest cities.

## Featured Locations

Each location in this collection has been carefully selected to represent a different aspect of New York's rich tapestry - from historical landmarks to cultural hotspots, natural spaces to architectural marvels.

Whether you're planning your first visit or you're a longtime resident looking to explore new corners of the city, these locations offer something special for everyone.
