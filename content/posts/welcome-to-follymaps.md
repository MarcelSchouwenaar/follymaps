# Welcome to Follymaps!

This is our first post featuring geolocation mapping capabilities. We're showcasing the iconic Central Park in Manhattan, New York City.

## About Central Park

Central Park is an urban park in New York City, located between the Upper West and Upper East Sides of Manhattan. It is the fifth-largest park in the city, covering 843 acres (341 ha). Central Park is the most visited urban park in the United States, with an estimated 42 million visitors annually.

## Features

- **Great Lawn**: Perfect for picnics and outdoor activities
- **The Mall**: A beautiful tree-lined walkway
- **Bethesda Fountain**: Iconic fountain and meeting place
- **Strawberry Fields**: Memorial to John Lennon
- **The Lake**: Perfect for boating and relaxation

The park offers a peaceful retreat from the bustling city life and provides countless opportunities for recreation, relaxation, and exploration.
